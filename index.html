Welcome to Pelsall Villa Colts F.C.

Welcome to our pitch booking system. Here you can book pitches for matches, training sessions, friendlies, tournaments, and other events at our EDC and Long Lane facilities.

EDC Facility

Our EDC facility features multiple pitches of different sizes:

  • 3 x 11-a-side pitches (full or half)
  • 3 x 9-a-side pitches (full or half)
  • 3 x 5-a-side pitches (full or half)

Long Lane Facility

Our Long Lane facility features:

  • Multiple pitches of various sizes
  • Grass and artificial surfaces
  • Flexible booking options
